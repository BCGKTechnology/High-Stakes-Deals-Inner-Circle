HubSpot integration

Optional. When enabled, each application is ALSO sent to HubSpot. Leaving this off changes nothing — the site keeps saving to Supabase as normal.

Where do I find these?

HubSpot → Marketing → Forms → create/select a form → Share → “Embed”. The portalId and formGuid are in that snippet. Map these HubSpot field names for full data: firstname, lastname, email, phone, goals, owns_real_estate, capital.
