Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BCGK High Stakes Deals ("BCGK," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information you provide when you visit our website, register for a webinar or event, apply to the Inner Circle Program, or otherwise interact with us. By using our website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a form on our website — for example, to register for a free webinar, apply to the Inner Circle Program, or request a call — we may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other details included in the form (such as your experience level, liquid capital range, or how long you've followed BCGK).

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our site, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and general usage data through cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5 below).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Register you for webinars, events, or the Inner Circle Program and send related confirmations, reminders, and calendar invitations;
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support;
  • Send you educational content, updates, and marketing communications by email and, where you've provided consent, by SMS text message;
  • Personalize and improve your experience on our website;
  • Analyze site usage and marketing performance; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

Email & SMS Communications

By submitting your email address or phone number through one of our forms, you consent to receive marketing and informational communications from BCGK by email and/or text message. Message and data rates may apply to SMS communications. You may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in each message, and you may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying "STOP." You may withdraw your consent to future communications at any time by contacting us at info@bcgk.com.

3. Consent

By voluntarily submitting your information through our website, you provide your consent for BCGK to collect, use, and disclose that information as described in this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will seek your express consent before using your information for purposes beyond those disclosed at the time of collection, such as certain marketing communications.

4. Third-Party Platforms & Service Providers

We use trusted third-party platforms to help operate our business, and your information may be processed by these providers on our behalf:

  • Zoom — hosts our live webinars and related event content;
  • HubSpot — processes form submissions and manages our customer relationship and email/SMS marketing communications;
  • Calendly — facilitates scheduling for one-on-one calls;
  • Investor Cafe — provides the secure investor portal for current BCGK investors, which is governed by its own separate terms and privacy practices.

These providers are only permitted to use your information as necessary to provide their services to us and are subject to their own privacy and security obligations.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to recognize your device, remember your preferences, measure site performance, and understand how visitors engage with our content and marketing campaigns. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect certain site functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. Our third-party platform providers maintain their own industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Age Requirement

Our website and services are intended for individuals who are at least the age of majority in their state or jurisdiction of residence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, or to ask a question about how your information is handled, please contact us at info@bcgk.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date, and continued use of our website after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us at info@bcgk.com.
