Not Everyone Is a Fit

Is the Inner Circle for you?

✓

This is for you if...

You own real estate beyond your primary residence and you're past the 10–50 door ceiling. You're willing to invest in mentorship today if it aligns with your 12-month goals. You have liquid capital or credit to move on deals. You want a room of operators actually closing right now — not a course you'll never finish.

✕

This isn't for you if...

You're brand new to real estate with no holdings yet — start with the free masterclass instead. You're looking for a passive investment, not an operating mastermind. You're not ready to invest in yourself or show up to the room. You want a get-rich-quick pitch instead of a real framework.
