About BCGK High Stakes Deals

Built by operators. Not theorists.

Founded in 2020, BCGK started with a single triplex. Today the team manages a ~$45–50M multifamily portfolio across the Greater Sacramento Metro — and the High Stakes Deals Inner Circle exists to bring the next wave of operators up with them.

Our Story

From one triplex to 250+ doors

BCGK is a multifamily real estate investment group specializing in the Greater Sacramento Metro area. What began as four partners pooling capital for a single triplex has grown into a portfolio of 120+ residential units and roughly $28M in assets under management — with a combined operator track record now touching 250+ doors and a ~$45–50M portfolio value.

The High Stakes Deals Inner Circle grew directly out of that experience. Every framework taught inside the community — sourcing, underwriting, financing, raising capital, and operating — is pulled straight from deals the team has actually closed, not theory.
